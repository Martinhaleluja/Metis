METIS — An AI Companion for Learning the Digital World by Doing

Built by Martin Nakasole


WHAT IT IS

Metis is a context-aware AI companion that helps people learn and confidently
use software, websites and digital tools while they are actually working with
them.

Most tools do one of two things: they explain a task somewhere else, in a
tutorial you have to translate back to your own screen, or they simply do the
task for you and leave you no better off. Metis does neither. It understands
what is on your screen, works out what you are trying to accomplish, and
guides you inside the application you are already using.

It can speak with you, explain unfamiliar tools, highlight buttons and
controls, show you where to navigate, notice mistakes, and suggest what to do
next as you work.


YOU DO THE WORK

Metis teaches; it does not take over. It explains what to do and why, points
at the control you need, and draws over the screen to show you where to look.
It never clicks, types, or moves the pointer for you, and there is no setting
that lets it. The skill stays with you.

Metis keeps a record of which steps you have already managed, and gradually
says less about the things you have proven you can do. Your progress is shown
on the Preferences dashboard.


IT WORKS WITHOUT AN INTERNET CONNECTION

Metis can run entirely on your own computer using Ollama, Whisper.cpp and
Piper. Nothing is sent anywhere, no connection is needed once the models are
installed, and no data is spent. Choose "Keep everything on this PC" during
setup.

Otherwise Metis uses a cloud model of your choosing (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or
OpenRouter) and you supply the API key.

OpenRouter gives access to many hosted models behind one key, including free
ones. Two things to know before relying on the free tier: they are rate
limited to roughly 20 requests a minute and a small number per day, and many
are served by providers that may log or train on what they receive -- which
for Metis means pictures of your screen. If the work must stay private, use
the fully local option instead.


WHAT IT CAN SEE

When you ask a question, Metis takes a picture of your whole desktop -- every
monitor -- and sends it to the AI provider you chose, so it can answer. On the
fully local setting it is processed on this computer instead and sent nowhere.
Metis does not watch continuously and it does not record.

You can turn screen capture off during setup or later in Preferences. Metis
will still answer questions; it just will not be able to see or point at
anything.

Metis also reads the names and labels of on-screen controls so it can point
accurately. It cannot see inside applications running as administrator.

API keys are stored in Windows Credential Manager, never in a settings file.


SAFETY

Metis never operates your computer. It cannot click, type, move the pointer,
or submit anything on your behalf, so nothing it does can delete, buy, pay,
send or submit. That limit is the design and cannot be configured away.


KEYS TO KNOW

  Ctrl+Shift+1      Hold to talk
  Ctrl+Alt          Ask about what is on screen
  Ctrl+Alt+Shift    Point at whatever is under the pointer
  F12               Stop Metis immediately

While Metis is running, F12 is reserved for stopping it, so other programs
will not receive that key.


Metis installs for the current user only and does not require administrator
rights.
